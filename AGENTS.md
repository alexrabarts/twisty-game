# Agent Guidelines

Twisty Game is a browser motorcycle racing game: classic scripts under `js/`, three r150, no build step. Project notes for agents live in `.agent/` (start with `.agent/README.md`).

## Build/Test Commands
- Install: `just deps` (the Firebase CLI through Homebrew if it is missing, then `npm ci` here and in `functions/`)
- Serve locally: `npm run serve` (http://localhost:8080)
- Run all tests: `node --experimental-vm-modules node_modules/.bin/jest`
- Run one file: `node --experimental-vm-modules node_modules/.bin/jest tests/unit/vehicle.test.js`
- Browser proofs: `node tools/terrain-check.mjs legs,pages` (`npm run test:integration`). Needs Chrome: set `TWISTY_CHROME` to a Chrome binary, pass `--chrome <path>`, or run `npx puppeteer browsers install chrome`
- The streamed world is the default ride: on the plain page, choosing a rider on the menu starts the Tremola slice; CLASSIC LEGS on the rider screen (or `?legs`) opens the procedural legs, and a world whose data does not load hands over to the legs with a note. `/?world=tremola` (`?world=<stage|all>`) starts a world without the menu. Only `js/ridemode.js` reads `?world`. `?worldData=<path>` reads the data from another folder (relative, under `world/` or `tests/fixtures/world/` only); a world read that way never submits a time. Browser proofs: `node tools/terrain-check.mjs world-window,world-ground,world-junction,world-wrongway,world-credit,world-net --world tremola`. Nothing closes a side road: `world-junction` fails on a barrier in the scene, a side road not drawn as its stub or a wall or fall the physics makes on a stub, and `tests/unit/world-side-roads.test.js` rides every side road at 10 m/s into the off-route rule ("Wrong way", then back on the road 10 s later; `.agent/system/world.md`, "A side road is open"). `world-frustum` rides every hairpin and fails when the bike leaves the chase view, `world-camera-walls` rides every hairpin in the standard and the high view and fails when a wall, a parapet, a house, the land or a road stands between the chase camera and the bike or in the view's near plane (`--off` rides without the rule; the rule: `.agent/system/world.md`, "Short of a wall"), `world-buildings` fails on a drawn building over a road band, `world-fairness` fails on a crash cause with nothing drawn there, a physics road band off the drawn ribbon, or a crash or restart in any hairpin at 5 and 7 m/s, `world-models` loads every `assets/**/*.glb` in the page, and `world-hairpin-shots --out <dir>` takes GPU pictures of the first three hairpins and the start, `world-place-shots [--ids <ids|all>] [--out <dir>]` takes GPU pictures of the stopping places (by default a layby, a viewpoint and the car park) from the chase view with the riders waiting in them and from across the road, with an `index.json` (the rules: `.agent/system/world.md`, "Stopping places"), `world-fall-shots --out <dir>` takes GPU pictures of the rider's fall of each kind at 6, 14 and 28 m/s, three moments each, from beside him (the rule: `.agent/system/handling.md`, "The rider's fall"), `world-group-shots` saves pictures of the group ride (the start, a straight, a hairpin, riders waiting in a stopping place) into `~/Library/Caches/twisty-world/group-shots/` with each rider's state (the rules: `.agent/system/group.md`), `world-haze` fails when distant land is not the colour of the photos by its distance (1 to 3 km, 3 to 8 km, from 8 km), when it is greener than the sky near the horizon, or when anything a world draws has no haze (`LandShade.HAZE`; `node tools/world/photomatch.mjs haze` measures the photos), `world-floating` fails on a drawn road edge over the land, land over the road, the physics ground off the drawn ground, a road's underside or a dark seam in the named views (`--out <dir>` saves them), `world-junctions` fails on a side road that does not meet the ride (a step or hole in its surface, land over it, a deck or parapet in its mouth, an open edge there, the ride's wall, coping or parapet drawn across its mouth, a place in its mouth where the physics makes the ride's wall band a wall, land, laid ground or the ride's verge drawn over it in its mouth (`strips`), a gap under the line between it and the ride beside it at one height (`pits`), or a narrow gap open to the land between it and the ride beside it at any heights (`clefts`); the builders' rules are in `.agent/system/world.md`, "A side road meets the ride", and every new world must pass it), `world-stones` fails on a marker stone that is neither on a wall's coping, beside a sharp drop as drawn nor on the outside of a hairpin, that is on a hairpin with no drop of 4 m behind it (`hairpinDropM`; a stretch needs 7 m, `dropM`), that is on the inside of a hairpin, that has a wall or a parapet drawn at its edge (off a wall), that stands on a wall with a parapet, off its coping or on a wall that drops 4 m or more (`wallMaxM`: on a chosen low wall a row of stones takes the parapet's place, whole walls only), or that is not in the drawn ground, and on a piece of the kerb with its foot over the drawn ground or away from every hairpin (the kerb is under a hairpin's row only) (the rule: `.agent/system/world.md`, "Marker stones stand at sharp drops, round hairpins and on wall tops"), `world-tunnels` fails on land in a tunnel's or a gallery's box, a gap in the ride's tube floor, land over the road in front of a portal or land over the roof behind a portal with nothing drawn to front it, and with `--out <dir>` takes GPU pictures of every portal (`tests/unit/world-tunnels.test.js`; the rule: `.agent/system/world.md`, "Land and the tunnels"), `world-watertight` fails on a gap in the drawn land seen from the helmet, chase or high camera every 50 m (a ray that passes under the land and meets nothing, a land underside or a skirt seen through it) or an apron or retaining wall whose foot stands over the land or over the ride's carriageway (`tests/unit/world-watertight.test.js` holds every shared tile edge closed at every pair of LODs); `node tools/world/verify.mjs floating` and `overlap` check the same in the data, and `node tools/world/verify.mjs spikes --stages tremola` (`tests/unit/world-spikes.test.js`) lists by kilometre every land triangle that stands up beside the ride as a spike or a tent, every wall end that is open, has an open side beside it (`flaps`) or is cut off at its height (`proud`: an end more than 1 m over the ground in front or with no end face; a wall past its end keeps its height and ends closed, `RoadMesh.edgeWorks` `taper`), every face of stone that stands out from a wall or is not laid as its wall's face (`fins`, `unfaced`), every piece of wall thinner than the wall (`thin`: a piece past a wall's end with less stone at its top than the wall's coping, or an upright face with nothing drawn at its top), every piece of a wall's top folded over itself or over the piece beside it (`folds`), every pair of walls side by side where one should go on (`jogs`: a closed end within 2 m of another road's wall, or a short joining piece set across between two walls) and every coping piece that stands out of the coping it meets or stands alone (`strays`), every wall's top less than `RoadMesh.WALL_MIN_UP_M` (0.5 m) over the road under it (`low`), every viaduct pier or face whose foot stands over the land or on a road (`piers`), every arch's underside with land over it (`soffits`; the viaducts' rule: `.agent/system/world.md`, "Arched viaducts") and every edge of laid ground over open air (the builders' rules are in `.agent/system/world.md`, "No land stands up beside the road, and a wall ends in a bank"). The importer and its cache rules are in `.agent/system/world-data.md`
- The bike on a world stands on the drawn ground: pitched to the ground under both tyres, its lean measured from the ground's normal, a foot put down on the ground at its own place (`Vehicle.groundPose`, `Vehicle.GROUND`; `.agent/system/handling.md`, "The bike stands on the ground"). `tests/unit/world-ground-contact.test.js` holds it on the steepest grade and land of each world; `node tools/terrain-check.mjs world-ground-shots --world tremola --gpu --out <dir>` takes chase and side pictures of the bike stopped up, down and across the steepest grade
- Debug mode: add `debug` to the page's address (`/?debug`, `/?world=tremola&debug`): every rider, bike and leg is open (`TourSystem` `unlockAll`; the saved progress is not changed), and N puts the bike 40 m before the next checkpoint of a world ride (`Game.debugSkipToNextGate`); a ride that skipped never submits a time. T opens the tuning panel while riding: a slider for each row of `Tuning.PARAMS` (`js/tuning.js`), each a multiplier on the default, kept in `localStorage` (`twistyTuning`) and read in debug mode only; Copy values gives the text (`twisty tuning v1`) that `Tuning.parse` reads; a ride that was tuned never submits a time (`.agent/system/handling.md`, "The tuning")
- The hill ride: the page with the address key `RideMode.HILL_KEY` (`/?<key>`, written in `js/ridemode.js` alone) opens a short descent of a cobbled village lane on a delivery bicycle (`js/hill/`), with no menu and no clock on screen: it is timed from the roll-off to the finish on the last left, and the end card shows the time, the best and its board (`village-hill-v1`, `js/hill/board.js`, `functions/hill-courses.json`). Each day starts with the boy pushing the bicycle on foot up to the bakery's cart, and on the way down Q and E throw bread at the doors that wait for it (`js/hill/day.js`); the legs come out at speed downhill. `tests/unit/hill-ride.test.js`, `node tools/terrain-check.mjs hill-shots --gpu`; rules in `.agent/system/hill.md`
- Graphics levels: one table in `js/quality.js` (`Quality.LEVELS`, lowest first; the top is the game as drawn before the ladder), each level a pixel ratio cap, a shadow map size or none, MSAA samples, bloom and a world's tree share. The start level comes from the device (`Quality.START`), then the frame time steps it (`Quality.RULES`); `game.quality.fix(level)` and `release()` hold a level or give it back to the frame time (the tuning panel's Graphics row in debug mode, where the HUD shows the level). `node tools/terrain-check.mjs world-quality --world tremola --gpu [--phone] [--measure] [--out <dir>]` measures each part of a world frame or times and pictures each level. Rules: `.agent/system/graphics.md`
- Rider modes (Rain, Road, Sport, Track): one table in `js/ridermodes.js` (`RiderModes.MODES`), each row a hairpin assist level and multipliers over the ids of `js/tuning.js`. Road is the bike with no mode, to the last bit. The control is on the menu and the pause panel, H steps the mode, and in a ride it changes only while the bike stands. The hairpin assist has no setting of its own. Rules: `.agent/system/handling.md`, "The rider modes"
- Dialogs: every dialog (menu, the classic legs' crash card, finish, pause, the world ride's confirm, credits, best times, name entry) opens through the one stack in `js/modals.js`, which keeps only the top one interactive and gives it every key; give a button its shortcut with `data-key="KeyM"`, never a `keydown` listener or a z-index of its own. `node tools/modal-check.mjs [--variant desktop|touch] [--out <dir>] [--gpu]` opens them in every reachable sequence and fails on a covered button, two interactive dialogs, focus outside the top one or a shortcut that does nothing; its `menu-*` sequences start on the plain page and ride the world, a classic leg and the fallback with real clicks. A crash in a world ride opens no dialog: the game restarts the rider in place after the fall (+5 s; `.agent/system/world.md`, "A restart is in place"), and Restart leg is on the pause panel
- Ride a leg in the console: inject `tools/autopilot.js`, then `twistyDev.start('mountain-dawn')` and `twistyDev.autopilot({ speed: 25 })`

## Terrain baselines
- `tests/golden/terrain-baseline.json` and `tests/golden/browser-baseline.json` define "unchanged" for the terrain work. They are captured once from untouched game source and never regenerated. Check them with `shasum -a 256 tests/golden/*-baseline.json`.
- `tests/golden/parts-baseline.json` is the mutable per-leg record; `tools/terrain-check.mjs` guards how it is rewritten.
- `tools/terrain-check.mjs` aborts every request to `cloudfunctions.net`; never point a page at production functions.

## Code Style
- Use British English for human-facing text, American English for code
- Single word variable names preferred; refactor if scope becomes too large
- No ASCII art dividers in comments; use single line comments with blank lines for paragraphs
- Document WHY not WHAT; avoid obvious comments

## Error Handling
- Handle errors explicitly; no silent failures

## General
- Don't apologize for mistakes; provide corrected response
- No deprecation TODOs; leave code in place
- Don't create README.md files unnecessarily
