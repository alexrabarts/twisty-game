# Agent Guidelines

Twisty Game is a browser motorcycle racing game: plain scripts under `js/`, three r150, no build step. Project notes for agents live in `.agent/` (start with `.agent/README.md`).

## Build/Test Commands
- Install: `just deps` (the Firebase CLI through Homebrew if it is missing, then `npm ci` here and in `functions/`)
- Serve locally: `npm run serve` (http://localhost:8080)
- Run all tests: `node --experimental-vm-modules node_modules/.bin/jest`
- Run one file: `node --experimental-vm-modules node_modules/.bin/jest tests/unit/vehicle.test.js`
- Browser proofs: `node tools/terrain-check.mjs <probe> --world <stage>` (the world probes below), `node tools/modal-check.mjs` and `node tools/home-check.mjs`. They need Chrome: set `TWISTY_CHROME` to a Chrome binary, pass `--chrome <path>`, or run `npx puppeteer browsers install chrome`
- The streamed world is the default ride: on the plain page, choosing a rider on the menu starts the world (the tour's map picks the leg). A world whose data does not load, or whose land stops loading during a ride, shows an error card on the dialog stack with Retry and Back to menu (`Game.showLoadError`, the texts in `RideMode`); no other ride takes over. The old procedural legs are gone from the game; `js/terrain.js`, `js/environment.js`, `js/course.js`, `js/world/legroute.js`, `js/cones.js` and `js/rockfalls.js` stay in the repository, not loaded by the page, only because `tests/unit/world-route.test.js`, `world-projection.test.js` and `world-verge.test.js` still build an old leg. `/?world=tremola` (`?world=<stage|all>`) starts a world without the menu. Only `js/ridemode.js` reads `?world`. `?worldData=<path>` reads the data from another folder (relative, under `world/` or `tests/fixtures/world/` only); a world read that way never submits a time. Browser proofs: `node tools/terrain-check.mjs world-window,world-ground,world-junction,world-wrongway,world-credit,world-net --world tremola`. Nothing closes a side road: `world-junction` fails on a barrier in the scene, a side road not drawn as its stub or a wall or fall the physics makes on a stub, and `tests/unit/world-side-roads.test.js` rides every side road at 10 m/s into the off-route rule ("Wrong way", then back on the road 10 s later; `.agent/system/world.md`, "A side road is open"). `world-frustum` rides every hairpin and fails when the bike leaves the chase view, `world-camera-chicane` rides the whole stage behind the pilot in the near and the far chase and fails on a chase camera that swings past its bounds through a chicane, or comes within 1 m of a tunnel's or gallery's roof or wall (the rule: `.agent/system/world.md`, "Calm through an S" and "Under a tube's roof"), `world-camera-walls` rides every hairpin in the near and the far chase and fails when a wall, a parapet, a house, the land or a road stands between the chase camera and the bike or in the view's near plane (`--off` rides without the rule; the rule: `.agent/system/world.md`, "Short of a wall"), `world-buildings` fails on a drawn building over a road band, `world-fairness` fails on a crash cause with nothing drawn there, a physics road band off the drawn ribbon, or a crash or restart in any hairpin at 5 and 7 m/s, `world-models` loads every `assets/**/*.glb` in the page, and `world-hairpin-shots --out <dir>` takes GPU pictures of the first three hairpins and the start, `world-tube-shots --out <dir>` takes GPU pictures inside every tunnel and gallery of the ride from the near chase and riding in and out (the rules: `.agent/system/world.md`, "The inside of a tube"), `world-place-shots [--ids <ids|all>] [--out <dir>]` takes GPU pictures of the stopping places (by default a layby, a viewpoint and the car park) from the chase view with the riders waiting in them and from across the road, with an `index.json` (the rules: `.agent/system/world.md`, "Stopping places"), `world-tree-shots [--at s,s] [--out <dir>]` takes GPU pictures of the trees at each station (the chase view, a close view of the nearest tree, a middle view) with the window's counts (the rules: `.agent/system/graphics.md`, "The trees' levels of detail"), `world-fall-shots --out <dir>` takes GPU pictures of the rider's fall of each kind at 6, 14 and 28 m/s, three moments each, from beside him (the rule: `.agent/system/handling.md`, "The rider's fall"), `world-group-shots` saves pictures of the group ride (the start, a straight, a hairpin, riders waiting in a stopping place) into `~/Library/Caches/twisty-world/group-shots/` with each rider's state (the rules: `.agent/system/group.md`), `world-haze` fails when distant land is not the colour of the photos by its distance (1 to 3 km, 3 to 8 km, from 8 km), when it is greener than the sky near the horizon, or when anything a world draws has no haze (`LandShade.HAZE`; `node tools/world/photomatch.mjs haze` measures the photos), `world-floating` fails on a drawn road edge over the land, land over the road, the physics ground off the drawn ground, a road's underside or a dark seam in the named views (`--out <dir>` saves them), `world-junctions` fails on a side road that does not meet the ride (a step or hole in its surface, land over it, a deck or parapet in its mouth, an open edge there, the ride's wall, coping or parapet drawn across its mouth, a place in its mouth where the physics makes the ride's wall band a wall, land, laid ground or the ride's verge drawn over it in its mouth (`strips`), a gap under the line between it and the ride beside it at one height (`pits`), or a narrow gap open to the land between it and the ride beside it at any heights (`clefts`); the builders' rules are in `.agent/system/world.md`, "A side road meets the ride", and every new world must pass it), `world-stones` fails on a marker stone that is neither on a wall's coping, beside a sharp drop as drawn nor on the outside of a hairpin, that is on a hairpin with no drop of 4 m behind it (`hairpinDropM`; a stretch needs 7 m, `dropM`), that is on the inside of a hairpin, that has a wall or a parapet drawn at its edge (off a wall), that stands on a wall with a parapet, off its coping or on a wall that drops 4 m or more (`wallMaxM`: on a chosen low wall a row of stones takes the parapet's place, whole walls only), or that is not in the drawn ground, and on a piece of the kerb with its foot over the drawn ground or away from every hairpin (the kerb is under a hairpin's row only) (the rule: `.agent/system/world.md`, "Marker stones stand at sharp drops, round hairpins and on wall tops"), `world-tunnels` fails on land in a tunnel's or a gallery's box, a gap in the ride's tube floor, land over the road in front of a portal or land over the roof behind a portal with nothing drawn to front it, and with `--out <dir>` takes GPU pictures of every portal (`tests/unit/world-tunnels.test.js`; the rule: `.agent/system/world.md`, "Land and the tunnels"), `world-watertight` fails on a gap in the drawn land seen from the near or the far chase camera every 50 m (a ray that passes under the land and meets nothing, a land underside or a skirt seen through it) or an apron or retaining wall whose foot stands over the land or over the ride's carriageway (`tests/unit/world-watertight.test.js` holds every shared tile edge closed at every pair of LODs); `node tools/world/verify.mjs floating` and `overlap` check the same in the data, and `node tools/world/verify.mjs spikes --stages tremola` (`tests/unit/world-spikes.test.js`) lists by kilometre every land triangle that stands up beside the ride as a spike or a tent, every wall end that is open, has an open side beside it (`flaps`) or is cut off at its height (`proud`: an end more than 1 m over the ground in front or with no end face; a wall past its end keeps its height and ends closed, `RoadMesh.edgeWorks` `taper`), every face of stone that stands out from a wall or is not laid as its wall's face (`fins`, `unfaced`), every piece of wall thinner than the wall (`thin`: a piece past a wall's end with less stone at its top than the wall's coping, or an upright face with nothing drawn at its top), every piece of a wall's top folded over itself or over the piece beside it (`folds`), every pair of walls side by side where one should go on (`jogs`: a closed end within 2 m of another road's wall, or a short joining piece set across between two walls) and every coping piece that stands out of the coping it meets or stands alone (`strays`), every wall's top less than `RoadMesh.WALL_MIN_UP_M` (0.5 m) over the road under it (`low`), every viaduct pier or face whose foot stands over the land or on a road (`piers`), every arch's underside with land over it (`soffits`; the viaducts' rule: `.agent/system/world.md`, "Arched viaducts") and every edge of laid ground over open air (the builders' rules are in `.agent/system/world.md`, "No land stands up beside the road, and a wall ends in a bank"). The importer and its cache rules are in `.agent/system/world-data.md`
- No land covers the road: `tests/unit/world-road-cover.test.js` streams every stage of every world folder (each leg ridden either way too) and reads the ride every 2 m, across the carriageway and 0.5 m past each edge every 0.5 m; it fails where the drawn land (the tile at full detail, the tile one level coarser where the ride can show it, the far grid) stands more than 2 cm over the drawn road outside a tunnel's or gallery's box, or where the physics under the carriageway leaves the drawn ribbon, and lists each place by stage, s, side and height. Its `KNOWN` table holds the counts that fail today and may only shrink (`.agent/system/world.md`, "No land covers the road"). It is the jest side of `world-floating`'s land over the road
- The physics stands on what is drawn beside the road: `tests/unit/world-ground-drawn.test.js` streams every stage every 300 m and reads the ride's verge and a wall's coping band every 0.2 m across, a quarter and three quarters of the way along each segment, each point located on its own leg; it fails where the height the bike stands at (`WorldGround.groundY`) differs by more than 0.2 m from the surface drawn there (a fall and a parapet-high wall are world-fairness's). Its `KNOWN` table holds the counts that fail today and may only shrink; `node tools/world/debug/ground-drawn.mjs <stage> [from] [to]` lists the places. The taper past a wall's end and the coping's band that the physics reads are the ones `RoadMesh.edgeWorks` lays (`plan.drawn`, `plan.band`; `WorldEnvironment.taperAt`, `copingAt`), its verge is the one drawn (`RoadMesh.verges`), on the verge the drawn verge is the top, and a tube's bench is only where both rows of the segment are a tube's (`WorldGround.roofedAt`)
- Where two roads meet: `tests/unit/world-meets.test.js` reads every junction node of every stage (the ride's, and every node of three side roads or more within 250 m of the ride, which free roam rides) from the drawn triangles within 30 m: land sunk or nothing between two roads less than 4 m apart (`bands`), a road's edge in the air over it (`ledges`), a hole a chase camera sees through (`see`), a wall's end standing more than 1 m proud (`proud`), a step or hole along a road that leaves the node (`steps`, `holes`), two roads' ribbons apart in each other's carriageway (`overlaps`), loose paving (`loose`), a road's line drawn over a carriageway with the right of way (`markings`) and land in the paving (`landIn`). Its `KNOWN` table holds the counts that fail today and may only shrink (`.agent/system/world.md`, "Where two roads meet"); `tests/helpers/world-meets.js`
- No far road hangs over a cleft: `tests/unit/world-far-edges.test.js` streams every stage (the bike every 1 000 m) and reads every edge of every road section drawn at level 1 at the rows it draws; where the edge stands more than 0.3 m over the land drawn there, rays from under the road out across the edge must meet its edge works. Its `KNOWN` table holds the open places left and may only shrink (`.agent/system/world.md`, "A far section's slope goes down to the land at every level")
- No tile edge of a world's land shows as a line: `node tools/terrain-check.mjs world-seams --world <stage> --gpu` puts the bike down every 250 m and reads the whole frame from the near chase camera, ahead and turned to each side, out to the far land. It fails on a skirt drawn darker than the land it hangs from, and on a line of a new tone where the drawn tiles meet the far land (`.agent/system/world.md`, "No tile edge shows as a line"). `world-look --script <file>` runs a script of your own in a world page and saves its pictures
- No sky shows through the far land, and no far grid's edge shows as a line: `node tools/terrain-check.mjs world-far-seams --world <stage> --gpu` puts the bike down every 100 m and reads the whole frame from the near and the far chase camera, ahead and turned to each side, out to the farthest land drawn (40 km). It fails on the sky under land in any column of the frame (a crack: the frame drawn again by kind over a black sky) and on a line of a new tone along the box of far.bin's grid or of a far level. `tests/unit/world-far-land.test.js` reads the same in the far grids of every world folder: each open edge of a grid under another grid, and the colour and normal the same either side of each grid's box (`FarLand.join`; `.agent/system/world.md`, "The far land is closed out to 40 km")
- The bike on a world stands on the drawn ground: pitched to the ground under both tyres, its lean measured from the ground's normal, a foot put down on the ground at its own place (`Vehicle.groundPose`, `Vehicle.GROUND`; `.agent/system/handling.md`, "The bike stands on the ground"). `tests/unit/world-ground-contact.test.js` holds it on the steepest grade and land of each world; `node tools/terrain-check.mjs world-ground-shots --world tremola --gpu --out <dir>` takes chase and side pictures of the bike stopped up, down and across the steepest grade
- The tour: js/world/tour.js is the one table of the owner's ten-leg tour, Airolo to Hospental (the menu's leg list, each stage's data folder, and the legs made from a ride a folder already holds, either way: `dir`). After an import, `node tools/world/tour.mjs` writes those legs' stages, stations and board rows into their folders; the world tests ride every built leg (`tests/helpers/world-stages.js`, `WORLD_STAGES=<id,id>` narrows them). Rules: `.agent/system/world.md`, "The tour" and "A stage ridden either way"
- The camera: two chase views, `Game.cameraModes`, the far chase (`far`, the default) and the near chase (`near`); Z (or the touch camera button) toggles them and `Game.setCameraView(type)` sets one. The choice is kept by type in `localStorage` `twistyCameraMode`; an index saved before (0 Onboard, 1 Helmet Cam, 2 Standard, 3 High View) reads as near for 2 and far for the others (`Game.cameraViewOf`). Probes name a view with `--modes near,far`, `--mode` or `--camera`
- Debug mode: add `debug` to the page's address (`/?debug`, `/?world=tremola&debug`): every rider, bike and world leg is open (`TourSystem` `unlockAll`; the saved progress is not changed), and N puts the bike 40 m before the next checkpoint of a world ride (`Game.debugSkipToNextGate`); a ride that skipped never submits a time. T opens the tuning panel while riding: a slider for each row of `Tuning.PARAMS` (`js/tuning.js`), each a multiplier on the default, kept in `localStorage` (`twistyTuning`) and read in debug mode only; Copy values gives the text (`twisty tuning v1`) that `Tuning.parse` reads; a ride that was tuned never submits a time (`.agent/system/handling.md`, "The tuning"); L in a world ride opens the free-fly camera (`js/world/freefly.js`, `Game.openFreeFly`): the ride holds still, the camera moves on six axes (WASD, R/F, the arrows or the mouse, Q/E roll, X level, Shift fast, Alt slow, the wheel steps the speed from 2 to 500 m/s, a gamepad too), the world streams round the camera, G keeps it above the ground, B puts the bike down on the ride nearest it, Escape or L gives the chase camera back; a flown ride never submits a time (`.agent/system/ui.md`, the free-fly camera; `tests/unit/free-fly.test.js`)
- The hill ride: the page with the address key `RideMode.HILL_KEY` (`/?<key>`, written in `js/ridemode.js` alone) opens a short descent of a cobbled village lane on a delivery bicycle (`js/hill/`), with no menu and no clock on screen: it is timed from the roll-off to the finish on the last left, and the end card shows the time, the best and its board (`village-hill-v1`, `js/hill/board.js`, `functions/hill-courses.json`). Each day starts with the boy pushing the bicycle on foot up to the bakery's cart, and on the way down Q and E throw bread at the doors that wait for it (`js/hill/day.js`); the legs come out at speed downhill. `tests/unit/hill-ride.test.js`, `node tools/terrain-check.mjs hill-shots --gpu`; the boy is one skinned body built from code (`js/hill/boy.js`, `js/hill/boymaps.js`; `tests/unit/hill-boy.test.js`, `node tools/terrain-check.mjs hill-look --gpu --script tools/hill-boy-look.js`); rules in `.agent/system/hill.md`
- Graphics levels: one table in `js/quality.js` (`Quality.LEVELS`, lowest first; the top is the game as drawn before the ladder), each level a pixel ratio cap, a shadow map size or none, MSAA samples, bloom and a world's tree share. The start level comes from the device (`Quality.START`), then the frame time steps it (`Quality.RULES`); `game.quality.fix(level)` and `release()` hold a level or give it back to the frame time (the tuning panel's Graphics row in debug mode, where the HUD shows the level). `node tools/terrain-check.mjs world-quality --world tremola --gpu [--phone] [--measure] [--out <dir>]` measures each part of a world frame or times and pictures each level. Rules: `.agent/system/graphics.md`
- Rider modes (Rain, Road, Sport, Track): one table in `js/ridermodes.js` (`RiderModes.MODES`), each row a hairpin assist level and multipliers over the ids of `js/tuning.js`. Road is the bike with no mode, to the last bit. The control is on the menu and the pause panel, H steps the mode, and in a ride it changes only while the bike stands. The hairpin assist has no setting of its own. Rules: `.agent/system/handling.md`, "The rider modes"
- Dialogs: every dialog (menu, finish, pause, the world ride's confirm, credits, settings, the error card of a ride that could not load, the hill ride's name entry) opens through the one stack in `js/modals.js`, which keeps only the top one interactive and gives it every key; give a button its shortcut with `data-key="KeyM"`, never a `keydown` listener or a z-index of its own. `node tools/modal-check.mjs [--variant desktop|touch] [--out <dir>] [--gpu]` opens them in every reachable sequence and fails on a covered button, two interactive dialogs, focus outside the top one or a shortcut that does nothing; its `menu-*` sequences start on the plain page and ride the world, and show the error card of a world that does not load (`menu-world-error`), with real clicks. A crash in a world ride opens no dialog: the game restarts the rider in place after the fall (+5 s; `.agent/system/world.md`, "A restart is in place"), and Restart leg is on the pause panel
- Model rounds: the `model-build` and `critic-panel` skills (`.claude/skills/`) improve a model in rounds of one change, judged by fresh critics on pictures; `tools/blender/README.md`, "Rounds on a model"
- `node tools/model-look.mjs --model <bike id | file.glb> [--rider <id>] [--outfit <id>] [--file <glb>] [--set k=v] --gpu --out <cache dir>` draws one model in the game's renderer and light at fixed views (three-quarter, side, rear, close-up, the menu, both chase views, the far crew); `--sheet <dir>=<label>,...` lays runs side by side
- `node tools/model-stats.mjs <picture dir> [--model <file.glb>]` measures each picture (clipped, crushed, luminance, saturation, flat tiles cut out as crops) and each model file (triangles and draw calls per level, bytes, texture bytes)
- Ride a world in the console: open `/?world=tremola`, inject `tools/autopilot.js`, then `twistyDev.autopilot({ speed: 25 })`

## Production
- `tools/terrain-check.mjs` aborts every request to `cloudfunctions.net`; never point a page at production functions.

## Code Style
- Use British English for human-facing text, American English for code
- Single word variable names preferred; refactor if scope becomes too large
- No ASCII art dividers in comments; use single line comments with blank lines for paragraphs
- Document WHY not WHAT; avoid obvious comments

## Error Handling
- Handle errors explicitly; no silent failures

## General
- Don't apologize for mistakes; provide corrected response
- No deprecation TODOs; leave code in place
- Don't create README.md files unnecessarily
